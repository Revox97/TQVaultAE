(#) Formats - MSH The mesh files are the 3d models loaded by the game. Both static (houses, sword, etc) and animated models (players, monsters, etc) use this format. **Chunk** The .msh is a binary chunk based format. After the msh file header there are one chunk after another until the end of the file. Each chink starts with a small ChunkHeader with a chunkId. The rest of the data in a chunk depends on the chunkId so all the chunkId are listed in it's own section below. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Chunk: uint chunkId; uint length; char data[length] HeaderMsh: uchar
