(#) Tutorial 14 - Steam Workshop In this tutorial we will explain how you can upload your mod to the steam workshop. # Open Steam Workshop tool The first thing you need to do is go to steam, press play on Titan Quest Anniversary Edition. After that a window with a few options will pop up. Select "Launch Titan Quest Mod Tools & Workshop" and press play. ![ ](guide_images/steamWorkshop/PopUp.png) # Create Project Now that you have pressed play a new window will pop up. This is the tool that will upload your mod to steam. ![ ](guide_images/steamWorkshop/steamWorkshop.png) 1. Press on "file" in the left upper corner. 2. Choose "Create New Project". ![ ](guide_images/steamWorkshop/createNewProject.png) 3. Choose a fitting name for your project and press "Create". # Upload Mod to workshop 1. Set a fitting title. 2. Set what type a visibility (defualts to hidden). 3. Set what type of tags that describe your project. 5. Press on the browse button. It will open a new file explorer window, just let it be we need it for later. ![ ](guide_images/steamWorkshop/browse.png) 6. Open a new file explorer window and go to "Documents\My Games\Titan Quest - Immortal Throne\custommaps" 7. Find you're mod and copy the folder over to the other window that we got when we pressed the browse button. 8. Close all the file explorer windows. 8. Add an image to the pathway that is shown in the box with the name "workshop_image.jpg", otherwise press the checkbox "Skip image upload". 9. Press "Create new item" button and your mod will soon be up on steam. # Updated you're mod on steam You just need to repeat the steps in "Upload the mod". # Options When you have created a new project their will be a few different option. 1. **Workshop item title** This is the title that everyone will see in the steam workshop. 2. **Visibility:** 1. Public: Eveyone will see it. 2. Friends: Your firends will be able to see your mode on Steam. 3. Hidden: This will only be shown to you and only you. 3. **Tags:** You can choose one or multiple tags to explain the mod. 4. **Workshop item description:** Adds an description for your mod on to steam 5. **Mod source folder:** This is the pathway for your mod 6. **Workshop image:** You can add a preview picture 7. **Change log:** Add a text that will explain what you have added or changed to your mod.
